## Result

The plan set two stops for the engineer. Both fire, so the engineer stops at the archive stop and the lead decides.

- A kept count fell below before. `task start` for 046/2.1 keeps 9 of 13 code and test lines, and kept 13 of 13 before. The task line of 046/2.1 is 1,903 characters. It is a requested block, so it sits in class 0 with the five cited requirements (5,264 characters). Class 0 takes 7,167 of the 8,000 characters. The four test lines `tests/query/budget.test.ts`, `change-cli.test.ts`, `plain.test.ts`, and `structural.test.ts` do not fit. The decisions 046/D4 to 046/D8 do not fit either. The old `task start` never printed the task line.
- A median rose more than 10 ms. The external `task start` 1.2 rose 12 ms in the paired run, and 12 ms in round 1. Its output grew from 238 to 1,627 characters. The paired medians of the repository rows move by 9 ms or less, which is inside the noise. The 228 ms of round 2 for REQ-LOOP-001 is noise. Its output is identical to the old one.

What `context` gains:

- `context src/graph/files.ts` keeps 2 of 2 lines where `bundle` kept 0 of 2.
- `context 045/2.1` keeps 2 of 2 where `bundle 045/2.1` kept 0 of 2.
- The external task that cites no requirement gets 1,627 characters from `task start`, and 238 before. `context` alone prints 1,384.
