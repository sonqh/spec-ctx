## Dogfood

- `docs/metrics/2026-10-24.md` is the first file of the series.

- The gate log caught failed pushes too. A typecheck error stopped two pushes before they reached GitHub.

## Decisions inside the spec

- `task reopen` writes its reason into the moved evidence as `reason`. Reopen files from before 005 have no reason, so `stale_catches` is a lower bound for them, as the design says.
- A change row of `debt` counts the debt of its own tasks: `done-without-evidence` and `manual-evidence`. The `total` row counts the full debt of REQ-STAT-004.
- A change with only reopened evidence still gets a row.
- `context_ratio` divides by the length of the `text` field of every canonical req, scn, and decision entry.
- `ci-local.sh` reads `SPECCTX_CI_STEPS_FILE` in place of its step list. Only the gate log test sets it.
