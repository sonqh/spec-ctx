## Finding for the bug intake

- With a `[scan] changes` glob that is not `openspec/changes/**`, the scanner names every change `main`, because the change name comes from the `/changes/<name>/` segment of the path. Two tasks `1.1` in two folders then share one ID. The lead found it on 2026-11-07 while adding a test for `fileEdges`. The archive check in `src/graph/files.ts` now uses `/archive/` as the scanner does. The glob finding goes to an issue.
