# Design

## Context

- `bundle()` in `src/graph/bundle.ts` adds a `decision for <REQ>` hit for a requested ID at depth 1, then for each cited requirement. Both loops skip a decision that `found` already holds. At `--depth 2` the walk of the first loop reaches a decision of a cited requirement at depth 2 through the back link of the requirement. The same happens with two requested IDs. The walk for ID A reaches the decision of ID B at depth 2 before the decision pass of B. The decision keeps that hit and its budget class 3. The test and code lines use an override rule: a depth 1 hit replaces a missing or deeper one.
- `resolveQuestion()` in `src/handoff/questions.ts` marks any open question resolved. `next` in `src/loop/next.ts` reads the stage of the question, not its answer. The plan stop (step 4b) ends on any question with `stage: plan`, open or resolved, and a started change skips it. The archive stop (step 9) approves on the newest resolved archive question after the newest evidence.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- A cited decision keeps depth 1 at every depth.
- An explicit verdict on plan and archive questions. A rejection holds the stop.

**Non-Goals:**
- `--since`, the benchmark, a version bump, and a verdict for other stages.

## Decisions

- D1 Layer 1. A `decision for <ID>` hit replaces the existing hit when there is none or when it is deeper than 1. This is the rule that the lines use near line 156. It applies in two places. The loop near line 151 of `bundle.ts` covers the cited requirements. The first loop near line 140 covers a requested ID. The walk for requested ID A then cannot leave the decision of requested ID B at depth 2. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-QRY-016
- D2 Layer 2. The verdict is two flags, `--approve` and `--reject`, on `handoff resolve`. Three forms were possible. A required verdict word, such as `yes` or `no`, makes the answer text a second place to say the same thing. A lead can write "no" as a sentence, or "ok, but not yet". A free text parse is the bug that this change removes. A flag cannot be misread. The CLI parses it and the answer text plays no part. `--approve` and `--reject` read as what they do, and no word list exists. The two flags exclude each other. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-HND-004
- D3 The CLI stores the verdict in the question as `verdict: approved` or `verdict: rejected`. `resolveQuestion()` takes the verdict and checks the stage. For `stage: plan` or `stage: archive`, a missing verdict or both flags throw a usage error with one line: `handoff resolve needs --approve or --reject for a plan or archive question`. The question stays open. A flag on a design question, a cap question, or a question with no stage also exits 2. Its one line says that the flags apply to plan and archive questions. The attempt-cap path of `task done` calls `resolveQuestion()` for stage `cap` with no verdict, so it does not change. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-HND-004, REQ-HND-002
- D4 The plan stop reads the newest question of the change with `stage: plan`. Newest means the highest question number `n`. While it is open, step 2 already blocks. A newest question with `verdict: approved` ends the stop. A newest question with `verdict: rejected` holds it, and `next` returns `ask` with `human: true`, state `plan_stop`, and the reason `plan rejected in question <n>: <answer>`. The command is the `handoff ask --change <c> --plan` command. A later rejection wins over an earlier approval. A resolved question with no verdict counts as NOT approved. The human asks again. The rejection check runs before the `started` guard of step 4b. A rejected newest question holds the stop even when a task of the change is `[x]` or current. A newest question with no verdict does not override that guard, so a change already under way is not stopped again. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-LOOP-009, REQ-LOOP-015
- D5 The archive stop reads the newest question of the change with `stage: archive` that a human resolved after the newest evidence. Newest means the highest question number `n`. With `verdict: approved`, `next` returns `archive` with `human: false`. With `verdict: rejected`, `next` returns `archive` with `human: true`, state `gate_passed`, the reason `archive rejected in question <n>: <answer>`, and the `handoff ask` command for a new archive question. With no verdict, the question counts as NOT approved. `next` returns `archive` with `human: true` and the `handoff ask` command. A later rejection wins over an earlier approval. New evidence lapses a resolve. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-LOOP-007, REQ-LOOP-015
- D6 The command that `next` prints for an open question depends on its stage. For `plan` and `archive` it is `handoff resolve <n> <--approve|--reject> "<answer>"` behind the run prefix. Other stages keep `handoff resolve <n> "<answer>"`. The skill text shows the verdict form in the `archive` action and the `ask` action. It says that the agent never resolves a plan or archive question, and the `plan` action says the same. Refs: ADR-007, REQ-LOOP-015
